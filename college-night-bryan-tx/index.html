A Change Of Scenery

Concrete Rodeo sits in Historic Downtown Bryan, about ten minutes from the Texas A&M campus and Kyle Field. It's a country dance hall, not a Northgate club, so College Night is an easy change of scenery, real dance floor, live bands and DJs, and a full bar. On big game days the screens run watch parties before the floor takes over, and if your crew has a birthday coming up, the hall does birthday parties too.
