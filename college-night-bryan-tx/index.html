College Night
in Downtown Bryan

Concrete Rodeo is 18+ after 9 PM with valid ID. All guests 21+ pay no cover. On College Night, guests 21+ with a valid college ID can get $1 well drinks. Check Instagram for the current night.

Admission, Cover and the Drink Special

18+ After 9 PM

Bring valid ID for admission. If you are 18 to 20, check that night’s cover before making plans.

No Cover for 21+

The 21+ no-cover policy applies with or without a college ID. It is separate from the drink promotion.

$1 Wells · 21+

The College Night well-drink special requires both age 21+ and a valid college ID. Admission at 18 does not include eligibility for this offer.

Planning a College Night Out

Send your group the same destination: 201 W 26th St, Bryan. Concrete Rodeo is in Historic Downtown Bryan. Check the events overview and that week’s Instagram announcement, then choose an arrival time and a ride home.

New to country dancing? Read what to expect at a beginner two-step lesson or the line-dancing guide. Those are separate programs; check the lineup rather than assuming a lesson is part of College Night.

For a birthday or a group that needs a reserved area, use the private-event inquiry before inviting everyone. An ordinary visit and a reserved event have different planning needs.

Before You Go

What night is College Night?
The current night is posted on Concrete Rodeo’s Instagram. Check the weekly announcement rather than relying on an old post.
Do I need a college ID to get in?
A college ID is required for the $1 well-drink special, not the 21+ no-cover policy. Bring valid ID for admission; guests 21+ claiming the special also need their valid college ID.
Can guests ages 18 to 20 attend?
Concrete Rodeo admits guests 18+ after 9 PM with valid ID. Guests ages 18 to 20 should check that night’s cover. The alcoholic drink promotion is restricted to guests 21+.
Is Concrete Rodeo on Northgate?
No. Concrete Rodeo is at 201 W 26th St in Historic Downtown Bryan. Use directions to the venue when planning your trip.
