Where the locals dance

Learn It On A Real
Dance Floor

This isn't a studio. It's a country dance hall, so you learn two-step right where everybody actually dances.

Concrete Rodeo is the old 3rd Floor Cantina in Historic Downtown Bryan, about ten minutes from the Texas A&M campus. Come early for the lesson, then stay for the band. The exact night lessons run moves with the weekly lineup, so follow our Instagram and you'll know when to show up. The same room also books for private events, birthdays, work parties, and full buyouts, all by email.
