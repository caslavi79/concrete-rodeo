Beginner Two-Step Lessons
in Downtown Bryan

Concrete Rodeo offers beginner two-step lessons at 201 W 26th St. You can come with a partner or on your own. Check the current lesson night and start time on Instagram before heading over.

What Is Two-Step?

Country two-step is a partner dance. One person leads and the other follows as the pair moves together around the floor. A first lesson is a chance to practice the basic rhythm and comfortable steps before adding turns.

Line dancing works differently: dancers follow a set sequence individually, usually in rows. You can enjoy both on a country night, but a two-step lesson and a line-dance routine teach different skills. See the line-dancing guide if you would rather learn a group routine.

For more background, the United Country Western Dance Council explains partner dance styles and line-dance choreography.

How to Prepare for Your First Lesson

  • Check the actual start time. Lesson nights follow the weekly lineup. Arrive before the lesson begins so you can get settled.
  • Wear something you can move in. Choose comfortable, secure footwear you have worn before. Bring your valid ID.
  • Start with the basics. Tell your partner you are learning, keep steps manageable, and leave room for the people around you.
  • Ask before joining someone. A dance invitation is optional. Accept a “no thanks” and give the other person space.

You do not need to master a routine before trying the dance hall.

Before You Go

Do I need dance experience?
No. Concrete Rodeo’s lessons are beginner-friendly. Start with the basics and let your partner know you are learning.
Can I come without a partner?
Yes. You can come solo or bring a partner. Two-step is a partner dance, so ask another dancer before joining them.
When are lessons and how much do they cost?
Check Concrete Rodeo’s current Instagram lineup for the lesson night, start time and any lesson charge. Check the latest details before heading out.
What are the age and cover rules?
Concrete Rodeo is 18+ after 9 PM with valid ID. Guests 21+ pay no cover; guests ages 18 to 20 should check that night’s cover. Door cover and a lesson charge are separate questions. Contact the venue about admission before 9 PM.
Where do the lessons happen?
At Concrete Rodeo, 201 W 26th St in Historic Downtown Bryan. Use the visit and directions section to plan your arrival.
