Concrete Rodeo is a country dance hall with no kitchen by design. On busy nights, food trucks set up out back, the bar pours everything else, and the dance floor does the rest. 21 and up after 9 PM.

No kitchen by design

No Kitchen, No Problem

Concrete Rodeo never set out to be a restaurant. There is no full kitchen and no printed food menu, and that is the honest frame, not a gap. The room is built for two-stepping, live bands, and a full bar, and the food is handled by the trucks that pull up out back when the night is busy. Nothing to reserve, no host stand, no menu to memorize. When a truck is parked, it gets announced on Instagram, and when there is not one, you can still line your stomach before you come and pull up for the floor. The hall sits at 201 W 26th Street in Historic Downtown Bryan, in the room that used to be the 3rd Floor Cantina.

How truck nights work

How Truck Nights Work

Truck nights follow the crowd, not a calendar. When the room is busy, a truck parks out back, and you can duck off the floor, grab something, and be back before the band starts the next song. There is no fixed weekly truck schedule to promise here and no rotating menu posted on this page, because the trucks change and the nights move with the lineup. The one reliable move is to check Instagram before you come, where the current week's nights and whoever is parked up get posted. Getting to the trucks is the same as any night downtown: free street parking around downtown Bryan after 6 PM, a lot out back, and rideshare drop-off out front on 26th Street.

Drinks are the house's job

Drinks Are The House's Job

Food might come from a truck, but the drinks are the house's job. Concrete Rodeo runs a full bar with beer, well drinks, whiskey, margaritas, and more, and there is no printed cocktail list to study. Tell the bartender what you are drinking and they will pour it, cash or card. The bar is the constant on any night, whether or not a truck is parked, and it is what keeps a long night going between songs. If you want the full picture of the room the bar sits in, the country dance hall page lays out the floor, the bands, and the rest.

Eat, drink, dance

A Bite From The Truck Is The
Intermission, Not The Headline

The headline is the floor. Food out back is just one more reason to stay a while.

Eat when you are hungry, drink when you are thirsty, dance the rest of the night. Live bands and late DJs take the room most weekends, there is space to two-step or line dance, and the patio is there for the in-between. This is the neon honky-tonk in the former 3rd Floor Cantina, and the truck out back is the easy fix for a long night, not the reason people come. Pull up, grab a plate if one is parked, and find your spot on the dance floor.
