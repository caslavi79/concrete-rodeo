Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Concrete Rodeo ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit concreterodeobcs.com (the "Site"). By using the Site, you agree to this Policy.

Information we collect

Information you provide. The Site does not use contact forms. If you reach out using an email, phone, or social link on the Site, we will have the information you choose to include in that message.

Information collected automatically. As with most websites, some information is collected automatically when you visit, such as your IP address, browser and device type, the pages you view, how you reached the Site, and your interactions with the Site. We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the Site. Google Analytics collects this usage information on our behalf. You can read how Google handles data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Cookies. The Site uses analytics cookies and similar technologies through Google Analytics. You can control or delete cookies in your browser settings.

How we use information

We use the information to respond to your inquiries, provide and improve the Site and our services, understand how the Site is used, keep the Site secure, and comply with the law.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us run and understand the Site, including Google as the provider of Google Analytics, our website host, and our email provider; and with authorities or others when required by law or to protect our rights, safety, or property.

Analytics and your choices

You can opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. Most browsers also let you refuse or remove cookies. Because there is no agreed standard for "Do Not Track" browser signals, the Site does not respond to them at this time.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you, and to ask how it is used. To make a request, contact us at concreterodeobryan@gmail.com, and we will respond as applicable law requires.

Children's privacy

The Site is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Third-party links

The Site may link to websites we do not control. This Policy does not cover those sites, and we are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Data security

We use reasonable measures to protect the information we collect. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date above. Continued use of the Site means you accept the updated Policy.
