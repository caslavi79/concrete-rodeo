What is this place

Part dive, part dance hall,
all Concrete Rodeo.

Same room you've always loved. We took over the old 3rd Floor Cantina, kept the vibe, and cranked the fun way up.

A few thousand feet of neon, the speakers pointed at the dance floor, and stiff pours behind the bar. Live bands and DJs that don't quit early. A patio for the in-between. No velvet rope, no attitude, just a real country dance hall in the heart of downtown Bryan. Pull up a stool.
