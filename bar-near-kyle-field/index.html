After The Game, Skip Northgate

When the game wraps at Kyle Field, most of the crowd files toward Northgate. Concrete Rodeo is the other direction: about ten minutes by car into Historic Downtown Bryan, a designated Texas Cultural District, in the room that used to be the 3rd Floor Cantina at 201 W 26th Street. It is a country dance hall, not a Northgate club, so it is an easy change of scenery for after the game. A real wood floor, live bands and late DJs, a full bar, and room to spread out without fighting the same lines. Come to catch the end of the game and stay because the room started moving.

Game day at the rodeo

Game Day At The Rodeo

On game days the screens run watch parties for the big ones, with the bar pouring and the room loud. The exact games and times live on Instagram, because sports schedules and bar lineups both move, and the honest promise is the simpler one: screens, a full bar, and downtown Bryan energy. When the lineup allows, a watch party can roll into live music or a late DJ set once the game is over, so the night does not have to end when the clock hits zero. Follow along on Instagram for the current week before you make the plan.

Getting here from campus

Getting Here From Campus

Getting here from campus is a short drive. Concrete Rodeo is about ten minutes by car from the Texas A&M campus and Kyle Field, straight into downtown Bryan. Parking is the easy part: free street parking around downtown Bryan after 6 PM, plus a lot out back, and rideshare drop-off out front on 26th Street. On a busy game night, a rideshare straight to the door on 26th Street beats circling for a spot, and it means nobody in the crew has to be the one watching the clock on drinks.

Final whistle to dance floor

The Game Got You Here.
The Floor Keeps You Here

A game at Concrete Rodeo does not have to end when the clock hits zero.

Live bands take the stage most weekends, late DJs keep it going with country-to-club crossover, and beginner two-step lessons run before the band if you want to learn a step. Cover varies by night and is posted at the door and on Instagram. And on College Night, a valid college ID gets you no cover and $1 well drinks all night, 21 and up after 9. The game is the reason you drove over; the floor is the reason you stay.

The Fine Print

The stuff folks ask before they drive over from campus.

About ten minutes by car. Concrete Rodeo is in Historic Downtown Bryan at 201 W 26th Street, a short drive from the Texas A&M campus and Kyle Field, which makes it an easy change of scenery after a game.
Concrete Rodeo runs watch-party nights for big games, with the games up on the screens and a full bar going. The exact games change week to week, so follow Instagram for the current lineup before you make plans.
There is free street parking around downtown Bryan after 6 PM, plus a lot out back. On busy nights rideshare is the easy call, and drop-off is out front on 26th Street.
Most nights are free to get in. Some headliner shows and DJ nights run a cover, always posted at the door and on Instagram. Cash and card both work.
Hours move with the weekly lineup, so there is no fixed closing time to promise. Concrete Rodeo posts the current week's nights and last call on Instagram, so check there for game-day timing.
After 9 PM, Concrete Rodeo is 21 and up with a valid ID, and the door cards everyone. Earlier in the evening can run a little more relaxed, so check Instagram if you are bringing a younger crowd.
