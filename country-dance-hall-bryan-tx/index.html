The Fine Print

The stuff folks ask before their first night at the hall.

Concrete Rodeo is a country dance hall and neon honky-tonk in Historic Downtown Bryan, in the old 3rd Floor Cantina at 201 W 26th Street. Big wood dance floor, live bands and late DJs most nights, beginner two-step, a patio, and a full bar. 21 and up after 9 PM.
We're at 201 W 26th St in the heart of Historic Downtown Bryan, Texas, in the room that used to be the 3rd Floor Cantina. About ten minutes from the Texas A&M campus and Kyle Field. Free street parking around downtown after 6 PM, plus a lot out back.
Not at all. Come early for beginner two-step lessons to learn the basics, then take the floor for line dancing or whatever the band's playing. Bring a partner or find one here.
After 9 PM, Concrete Rodeo is 21 and up with a valid ID, no exceptions. We card at the door, so keep your license handy. Earlier in the evening can run a little more relaxed, so check our Instagram if you're bringing a younger crowd.
Most nights are free to get in. Some headliner shows and DJ nights run a cover, and it's always posted at the door and on our Instagram. Cash and card both work.
We generally run Wednesday through the weekend, but the hours move with the lineup, so we post this week's nights and last call on Instagram. Give us a follow and you'll always know when the lights are on.
