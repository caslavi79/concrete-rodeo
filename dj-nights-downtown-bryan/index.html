Before you head down

The Fine Print

The stuff folks ask before they pull up to Concrete Rodeo.

Yes. Concrete Rodeo runs late DJ nights with country-to-club crossover, usually tied to the weekly lineup posted on Instagram.
The lineup changes, so do not trust a stale calendar. Follow @concrete_rodeo_ on Instagram for this week's DJs, bands, hours, and cover notes.
Cover varies by night. Some nights are free to get in, and some headliner or DJ nights run a cover that is posted at the door and on Instagram.
After 9 PM, Concrete Rodeo is 21 and up with a valid ID. Bring your license and expect to be carded at the door.
The site can honestly say country-to-club crossover. Specific DJs, set lists, and theme nights should come from Instagram when they are actually published.
Concrete Rodeo is at 201 W 26th St in Historic Downtown Bryan, in the former 3rd Floor Cantina room.
