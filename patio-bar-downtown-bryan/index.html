The Fine Print

The stuff folks ask before they pull up to Concrete Rodeo.

Yes. Concrete Rodeo has a patio where you can step out and cool off between songs.
Patio and pet policies can shift with the season and the night. Check Instagram before bringing a pup so you are working from the current answer.
Concrete Rodeo has a full bar and patio space, but any night-specific patio rules should come from staff or Instagram when you arrive.
Do not treat the patio like a fixed-hours promise. The weekly lineup and current operating notes are posted on Instagram.
Concrete Rodeo is at 201 W 26th St in Historic Downtown Bryan, in the former 3rd Floor Cantina room.
