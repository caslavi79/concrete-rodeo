The Fine Print

The stuff folks ask before they pull up to Concrete Rodeo.

Yes. Concrete Rodeo runs watch-party nights for big games, with screens, a full bar, and downtown Bryan energy.
Specific games change week to week. Follow Concrete Rodeo on Instagram for the current lineup before you make plans.
Yes. Concrete Rodeo is in Historic Downtown Bryan, about ten minutes from the Texas A&M campus and Kyle Field.
The bar and dance floor are first-come, first-served. For a big group or reserved area, email concreterodeobryan@gmail.com with your date and headcount.
Concrete Rodeo does not have a full kitchen or printed food menu. Food trucks may appear on busy nights, and Instagram is the source for who is parked out back.
After 9 PM, Concrete Rodeo is 21 and up with a valid ID. Earlier timing can vary, so check Instagram if you are coming before the late-night age rule starts.
