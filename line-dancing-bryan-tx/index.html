Concrete Rodeo · Downtown Bryan

Line Dancing
In Bryan, TX

Concrete Rodeo gives Bryan a real wood floor for line dancing, two-step, live bands, late DJs, and the kind of night that starts with one song and turns into last call. 21 and up after 9 PM.

On the floor

Move First, Ask Later

You do not need to know every count to get on the floor. Come early, watch the first few rounds, grab the beat, and jump in when the room starts moving.

Line Dance Energy

Country songs, dance-floor favorites, and enough room to move with the crowd.

Beginner Friendly

New to it? Come early for two-step basics and learn by watching the floor.

Full Bar
