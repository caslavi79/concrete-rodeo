The Fine Print

Everything folks usually ask us. Still stuck? Holler at us on Instagram or by email, links are at the bottom.

Getting In
After 9 PM, Concrete Rodeo is 21 and up with a valid ID, no exceptions. We card at the door, so keep your license handy. Earlier in the evening can run a little more relaxed, so check our Instagram or message us if you're bringing a younger crowd.
Most nights are free to get in. Some headliner shows and DJ nights run a cover, always posted at the door and on our Instagram. Cash and card both work.
College Night is our weekly deal: show a valid college ID for no cover and $1 well drinks all night. Bring the whole dorm. 21 and up after 9 PM. Follow our Instagram for the night it runs each week.
Come as you are. Boots, sneakers, whatever. We just ask: no offensive graphics, no full-face coverings at the door (so we can ID you), and shirts and shoes stay on. Theme nights are encouraged.
Getting There
Free street parking all over downtown Bryan after 6 PM, plus a lot out back. On busy nights, rideshare is your friend, and drop-off is right out front on 26th Street. Get directions →
We're in a historic downtown Bryan building, so if you have specific accessibility needs, message us on Instagram before you come. We'll tell you exactly what to expect and help you plan your visit.
The Night Itself
The bar and dance floor are first-come, first-served, no reservations needed. For big groups or a reserved area, email us and we'll sort it out.
Absolutely. Birthdays, work parties, full buyouts, you name it. Email concreterodeobryan@gmail.com with your date and headcount and we'll get you set up.
We're a country dance hall, so there's no full kitchen or menu. We run food trucks out back on busy nights, so check our Instagram to see who's parked up. Otherwise, come hungry to dance and we'll keep the drinks coming.
Yes, there's a patio to step out and cool off between songs. Patio and pet policies can shift with the season and the night, so check our Instagram for the latest before you bring a pup.
Left your card on a tab or lost a jacket on the dance floor? DM us on Instagram or ask the bar next time you're in. We hold lost-and-found behind the bar for two weeks.
