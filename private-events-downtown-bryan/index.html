The space

A Real Country
Dance Hall

The old 3rd Floor Cantina, turned into a neon honky-tonk you can rent out for a night.

Full bar, a big wood dance floor, room for a band or a DJ, and a patio to step out. It's in the heart of Historic Downtown Bryan, about ten minutes from the Texas A&M campus, with street parking around downtown and a lot out back. It's a real country dance hall, so tell us what you're picturing and we'll help make it loud. If the occasion is a birthday, the birthday party page has that rundown.
