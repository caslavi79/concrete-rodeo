Reserved Area or Full Buyout?

A reserved area is worth asking about when your group wants a meeting place during a normal night. A full buyout is a separate inquiry about using the hall for a private gathering. Availability and arrangements need to be confirmed for your date.

The hall has a wood dance floor, full bar and patio. If music, food, a particular layout or access needs matter to your plans, include those questions in the first email. A room feature does not mean entertainment or service is included in a rental quote.

For occasion-specific ideas, see birthday planning at Concrete Rodeo. For the room’s regular use, read the country dance hall overview.

What to Put in Your Inquiry

  • Preferred date and an alternate date, if you have one.
  • Occasion, estimated guest count and guest age range.
  • Preferred start and finish times, including any setup time.
  • Whether you want a reserved area or a full buyout.
  • Food, music, layout and accessibility questions.

Ask for the capacity and a complete quote for your date, including any deposit, cancellation, staffing or cleanup terms.

Email concreterodeobryan@gmail.com. Your date is reserved only after the venue confirms the arrangements.

Before You Go

How do I ask about a private event?
Email concreterodeobryan@gmail.com with your date, headcount, occasion and preferred times. The venue will confirm whether the proposed arrangement is available.
Are full buyouts and reserved areas available?
Both are offered by request. Ask about your date and group size; sending an inquiry does not confirm a reservation.
Where is Concrete Rodeo?
At 201 W 26th St in Historic Downtown Bryan, in the former 3rd Floor Cantina room. See directions and arrival information.
What if my group includes guests under 21?
The regular admission rule is 18+ after 9 PM with valid ID. Include your guest age range and timing in the event inquiry so the venue can address your specific plans.
Is there a phone number?
Yes, (281) 832-1482. Email is the primary private-event inquiry path so you can include the date, headcount and planning details in one place.
